## What it offers

**Browse**
- Home rows in your server's order, with a hero that follows the title you focus. Reorder or hide rows.
- Movies and Series grids with library, sort in either direction, filters and an A–Z jump, plus each library's Recommended rows and Collections.
- For You, Calendar, Watchlist, Favorites, History, and Requests when your server offers them.
- Search with people, and person pages with their filmography.
- Title pages with seasons and episodes, cast, trailers and extras, related titles and the full synopsis.

**Watch**
- Plays original files directly, and has your server convert what your device can't play.
- Choose the version, audio and subtitles before you press play. Your choices are saved to your Silo profile, so they follow you to the other Silo apps.
- Skip Intro, Recap and Credits, Up next with a countdown, and Are you still watching?
- A player menu on the remote's Menu key: info, stats, quality, speed, aspect, sleep timer, audio, subtitles (including online search, AI translation and sync) and chapters.
- Lowers quality automatically when playback keeps buffering, and tests your connection speed.
- Theme music on title pages.

**Your profiles and this device**
- Sign in with an activation code or your username and password.
- Household profiles, including PIN-protected ones, and profile management.
- Settings for your profile and for this device, and switching between several Silo servers.
- Optionally opens when Kodi starts, and asks before exiting so Back can't close it by accident.

**Extras**
- Silo rows, Watchlist and Favorites on Kodi's own home screen with the optional **Founders Theater Widgets** add-on.

## Install in Kodi

1. Open **Settings → File manager → Add source**, enter `https://slimshizn.github.io/Silo-Theater/` and name it **Founders Theater**.
2. Open **Add-ons → Install from zip file → Founders Theater**, and allow unknown sources if Kodi asks.
3. Select **repository.silo.theater-1.0.2.zip**.
4. Open **Install from repository → Founders Theater Repository** and install **Founders Theater**.

Open Founders Theater and sign in with your server address and Silo account. Updates arrive through the repository. For home screen widgets, also install **Founders Theater Widgets** from the same repository (**Video add-ons**).
